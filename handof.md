KNOWN BEHAVIOUR - measured, not bugs, but they surprise people
- "unlimited" really means "retry the same hop up to 100 times". It applies to every nvidia hop, any limitType "unlimited", retryAsUnlimited, and Literouter hops with no dailyCap. Measured with the same fake 503: Google = 1 call, gave up; NIM GLM-5.3 = retries until the 100-attempt cap (~6.4 min of backoff, 250ms doubling to 4s). A plain 429 is retried exactly like a 503, ignoring Retry-After. glm-5.3-nv has timeoutMs 30 min; the 45s/10-min retry window is only checked BETWEEN attempts, so one hung attempt can hold a hop up to 30 min.
- QUOTA outranks 429 in classifyError (patterns match on any status and include broad words: billing, daily limit, requests per day, resource_exhausted). A quota-worded 429 stops retrying at once, STILL falls back to the next hop, and is returned to the client as 429 (not 503). Pinned by two tests.
- Retry only applies before a stream starts; a stream that dies mid-way ends with finish_reason "length", it is not retried.
